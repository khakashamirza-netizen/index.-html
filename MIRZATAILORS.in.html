How to measure

Take these six measurements in inches over a thin shirt and send them to us on WhatsApp. Or visit the shop and we will measure you.

  • LengthFrom the shoulder seam down to where you want the kurta to end.
  • ChestAround the fullest part, under the arms. Keep the tape level.
  • ShoulderAcross the back, from one shoulder point to the other.
  • SleeveFrom the shoulder point down to the wrist bone.
  • NeckAround the base of the neck, with one finger of room.
  • WaistAround your natural waist, over the shirt.

How to order

  1. Send your measurements on WhatsApp, or visit the shop.
